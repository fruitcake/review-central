# Appearance

Every panel has the same look: the type, the square corners and the layout are the same for everyone. What changes is the name, the logo and the colours.

## The installation

Under **Admin → Settings**, **Appearance** sets for the whole installation:

- **App name**: shown in the sidebar, the page titles and the mail.
- **Logo**: an SVG or a PNG of at most 200 KB, shown in place of the mark and used as the favicon. It is kept in the settings, so nothing has to be served for it.
- **Primary colour**: the buttons, the active menu item and the mark. The sidebars take a deep shade of it, and text on it switches between white and dark by itself, so every colour stays readable. Empty is the default teal.
- **"developed by Fruitcake"**: a small credit at the foot of the sidebar, which can be turned off.

## A tenant

A tenant's panel can wear the team's own colours, over the installation's. On the tenant in the admin panel, **Colours** takes three:

- the **ink**: the sidebar, the text and the other dark surfaces;
- the **accent**: the active menu item and the brand mark;
- the **main button**, which follows the accent when left empty.

The shades that go with them, such as a hover, a tint and whether black or white goes on top, are worked out so the result stays readable. Empty is the installation's.

An invitation to someone who belongs to one tenant is mailed in that tenant's colours. The sign-in pages, the admin panel and other mail keep the installation's.
